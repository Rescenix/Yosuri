# Design: Minimax
# Type: general

> MiniMax's website is a clean, product-showcase platform for a Chinese AI technology company that bridges consumer-friendly appeal with technical credibility. The design language is predominantly white

  Brand Deep: #17437d

  Font: DM Sans

--- Components ---
  · Pill Primary Dark
